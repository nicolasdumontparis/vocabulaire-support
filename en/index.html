Vocabulary with Renardo

Learn languages through play

About the app

Renardo, a curious little fox, helps children aged 6 to 8 learn their first words in a new language. Everything is spoken aloud with natural recorded voices, so no reading is needed, and every answer takes a single tap. Explanations are spoken in the child’s own language.

  • 13 languages to learn
  • 300 words, 30 adventures
  • 100 friends to meet
  • No ads, no data collected

Frequently asked questions

How does the free trial work?

Everything is open for 7 days from the first launch. After that, missions and “Renardo forgot!” need the full version, while the map, “My words”, “My friends” and the “For grown-ups” area stay open. Deleting and reinstalling the app does not restart the free week.

How do I unlock the full version?

Open “For grown-ups” (the lock button on the map), or tap “Ask a grown-up” when the offer appears. After the parental check (Face ID, Touch ID, the device passcode or a 3-second press), tap Unlock. It is a single purchase with no subscription; the price shown is your local App Store price. With Ask to Buy, the purchase waits for the family organizer’s approval.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open “For grown-ups” and tap “Restore my purchases”, signed in with the Apple Account used for the purchase. The purchase is linked to that Apple Account and is not shared through Family Sharing.

How do I change the language learned?

Tap the language button on the map, or go to “For grown-ups” › Settings › Language learned. 13 languages are available, and each one keeps its own words and friends. The help language (for explanations) and the interface language are also in Settings; the language learned must differ from the help language.

What is in the “For grown-ups” area?

The words discovered and remembered over time, “Words to practise”, the week’s activity, the state of the trial or purchase, the language and sound settings (spoken words, sound effects, vibrations, gentle motion) and the children’s notebooks: create one per child, switch, reset or delete.

Is progress saved or synced?

Progress is saved only on the device. It is not sent anywhere and is not synced between devices. Deleting the app or a child’s notebook erases it.

There is no sound.

Check the volume and the silent mode, and make sure “Spoken words” is on in “For grown-ups” › Settings. All voices are included in the app, so no connection is needed.

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything is included in the app. A connection is only needed to buy or restore the purchase.
