Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

In short

Vocabulary with Renardo collects no data. There is no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking, and the app sends nothing to the developer or to anyone else.

What stays on the device

The first name or nickname typed when creating a notebook, the settings and the learning progress (words seen, answers and how long they took, friends met) are stored only on the device, to run the app and adapt the reviews. They never leave the device. You can erase them in “For grown-ups” (reset or delete a notebook) or by deleting the app.

Free trial

To count the 7-day free trial, the date of the first launch is kept in the device’s Keychain. It stays on the device, contains nothing about the child, and may remain after the app is deleted so that the trial does not start again.

Purchases

The in-app purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The developer receives no payment or personal details; the app only checks with Apple, on the device, whether the purchase has been made. Apple’s privacy policy applies to the purchase.

Device permissions

Face ID, Touch ID or the device passcode are only used for the parental check, through iOS: the app never receives any biometric data. The app does not use the camera, the microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Children

The app is made for children aged 6 to 8 and belongs to the Kids category of the App Store. It contains no advertising, no third-party analytics, no links out of the app and no way to communicate with others. Purchases and settings are behind a parental check. As no personal information is collected, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDK. Fonts, illustrations and voices are files included in the app and need no connection.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with its date.
